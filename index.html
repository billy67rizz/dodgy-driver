Road Dodge

Dodge. Upgrade. Repeat.

Floor it through a sunny highway. Dodge cones, barriers, tyres and traffic, collect points, and build the ultimate machine.

How to Play

Steer

A / D (or ← →) move left and right.

Speed

W accelerate for bonus points, S brake to get through tight gaps.

Survive

Crashes throw sparks and cost health. At zero, your car explodes. Esc pauses.

Upgrade

Spend points on cars, armour and unlimited upgrades. Better cars earn a points multiplier. Everything is saved in your browser.

Events

Rare random events boost your points multiplier for a short time. Grab the bonus!
